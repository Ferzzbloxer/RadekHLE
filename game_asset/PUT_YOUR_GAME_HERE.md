Put your own `.ipa` file in this folder, named exactly `game.ipa`
(`game_asset/game.ipa`). The build workflow checks for it and will fail
loudly if it's missing, rather than silently building a picker-only APK.

Only put something here that you actually have the rights to bundle and
redistribute this way (something you or your friend made, or a genuinely
open-source / freeware iOS title).
